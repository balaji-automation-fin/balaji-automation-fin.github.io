Finance Shorts Uploader

A private publishing tool for the YouTube channel Fin Know.

Finance Shorts Uploader is a personal automation tool used only by the owner of the Fin Know YouTube channel. It creates short educational videos about personal finance, investing and markets, and uploads them to that one channel through the YouTube Data API.

The tool is not offered to the public, and no other users sign in to it.

Videos are educational only and not financial advice. Each upload is labeled as containing AI-generated (synthetic) content.
