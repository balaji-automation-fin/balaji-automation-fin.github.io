Privacy Policy

Finance Shorts Uploader · Last updated: October 24, 2026

Who uses this app

Finance Shorts Uploader is a personal tool operated by the owner of the YouTube channel Fin Know. It is not available to the public. The only Google account that authorizes it is the channel owner's own account.

What Google data the app accesses

With the owner's permission, the app uses the YouTube Data API with these scopes:

  • youtube.upload: to upload videos to the owner's channel.
  • youtube: to read the channel name and confirm the upload settings of videos the app uploaded (for example, the AI-generated content label).

The app does not read, collect or store any data about viewers, subscribers, comments or any other users.

How data is stored

The OAuth access token is stored only on the owner's private server, in a file readable only by the owner's account. It is used only to upload and manage the owner's own videos.

Sharing

No Google user data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Use of YouTube is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access stops all uploads immediately. The stored token is then deleted from the server.
